# crates/app: the Make workbench (`make.rs`)

What the fab needs, on one page. Parent: `ui.md`.

- `make_page` — `summary` (a ring of the `checks` passed, what is left in a sentence, Export),
  then `cards` three to a row, as tall as the tallest's content last frame.
- `make_checks` — ERC, the board `behind` its schematic (`refresh_behind` runs F8 on a copy once
  edits rest 1 s), outline, ratsnest, DRC, fills, part numbers; each lists what it is about, with
  its fix; Fill in shows the first part lacking a number in the schematic.
- `make_files` (sizes once written; Show opens the folder), `make_order` (a quote's numbers;
  `FABS`' order pages by `open_url`: the system's opener, a new tab in the browser), `make_look`
  (`picture3d`; Make uploads the 3D scene as 3D does), `make_assemble` (the BOM checklist's top).
